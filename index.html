The firehouse

Built 1889. Pouring since 2005.

The C.H. Evans Hook & Ladder firehouse went up in 1889, designed by architect Michael O’Connor. It was named for Cornelius H. Evans, the brewer behind Evans Cream Ale and mayor of Hudson from 1868 to 1872.

The company kept the building until Hudson’s companies consolidated in 2002, partly because it bought a truck too large to fit in the firehouse. The original mahogany mantle and bronze chandelier are still upstairs. Since 2005 it has been the Spotty Dog, run by “a bonafide descendant of the Evans’ clan.”
